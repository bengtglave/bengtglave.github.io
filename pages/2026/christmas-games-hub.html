Christmas Mini Olympics

45–60 min • Teams of 2–3 or individual • 8 events

⌄

How to Play

  1. Decide whether you are playing in teams of 2–3 or individually.
  2. Rotate through all 8 events.
  3. Explain each event before starting.
  4. Record points after every event.
  5. Highest total score at the end wins.
Scoring
5 pts = 1st
4 pts = 2nd
3 pts = 3rd
2 pts = 4th
1 pt = 5th
Tie-breaker
One head-to-head challenge of your choice. Ornament Spoon Race works well.

Setup

  1. Gather supplies for all 8 events.
  2. Set up stations with a clear start/finish area.
  3. Decide teams or individual play.
  4. Read the rules for each event before starting.
1. Snowball Toss
Toss 5 soft snowballs into buckets. Closer buckets are worth more points: 1, 2, or 3 points each.
2. Candy Cane Hook
Using one candy cane in your mouth, pick up as many candy canes as possible in 60 seconds. No hands.
3. Bow Toss
Using a spatula, put bows into a container while blindfolded for 30 seconds. Most bows in wins.
4. Ornament Spoon Race
Carry an ornament or ping-pong ball on a spoon around a chair and back. Drop it and start over. Fastest time wins.
5. Jingle Bell Transfer
Move as many jingle bells as possible from one bowl to another using a spoon in 30 seconds.
6. Christmas Cup Tower
Build a 5-4-3-2-1 pyramid with 15 cups, then take it down. Fastest time wins.
7. Rudolph Nose
Put a dab of lotion or petroleum jelly on your nose and transfer red pom-poms from one plate to another. No hands. Most in 60 seconds wins.
8. Christmas Trivia Finale
Answer 10 holiday questions as a team. 1 point each.

Supply List

  • Soft snowballs or balled socks
  • Buckets or containers
  • Candy canes (10–15 per game)
  • Bows (10–15 per game)
  • Spatula
  • Container or bucket
  • Ornament or ping-pong balls
  • Spoons (2–3)
  • Jingle bells (15–20)
  • Plastic cups (15 per game)
  • Red pom-poms
  • Petroleum jelly or lotion
  • Timer
  • Trivia question cards, optional

Jingle Bell Challenge

20–30 min • Teams of 2–3 or individual • 6 rounds

⌄

How to Play

  1. Choose teams of 2–3 or play individually.
  2. Play one station at a time or set up all stations if space allows.
  3. Rotate through all 6 challenges.
  4. Record points after every round.
Scoring
3 pts = 1st
2 pts = 2nd
1 pt = 3rd
Tie-breaker
One Bell Drop rematch between tied teams.

Setup

  1. Gather supplies for each round.
  2. Set up one station at a time or all stations.
  3. Decide teams or individual play.
  4. Explain rules before each round.
1. Bell Shake
Put jingle bells inside an empty tissue box tied around the player’s waist. Shake until all bells are out. No hands. Fastest time wins.
2. Bell Scoop
Move as many jingle bells as possible from one bowl to another with a spoon in 30 seconds.
3. Bell Blow
Use a straw to blow jingle bells across the table and over the finish line. Fastest player wins.
4. Bell Balance
Balance jingle bells on a spoon while walking around a chair and back. Drop one and restart. Fastest time wins.
5. Bell Drop
Drop jingle bells from chest height into cups or containers. Different distances can be worth different points.
6. Bell Tower
Stack small cups or containers with one jingle bell balanced between each layer. Tallest stable tower wins.

Supply List

  • Jingle bells (20+)
  • Empty tissue box
  • Ribbon or belt to tie on box
  • Bowls (2)
  • Spoons (2–3)
  • Straws
  • Plastic cups or small containers
  • Chair
  • Timer
  • Score sheet

The Great Christmas Challenge

20–45 min • 3–5+ players • Secret missions all day/evening

⌄

How to Play

  1. Each player secretly draws 3 mission cards.
  2. Read your own missions quietly. Do not show anyone.
  3. Try to complete your missions during the game window without getting caught.
  4. If someone correctly calls out what mission you are attempting while you are doing it, that mission fails.
  5. Keep completed missions face down until the reveal.
  6. At the end, reveal completed missions and count points.
Winning
1 point per completed mission. Most points wins.

Optional: Hard missions = 2 points.
Host Tip
Mix easy, medium, and silly missions. The fun is the sneaking, not perfect strategy.

Setup

  • Print or cut mission cards.
  • Give each player 3 cards.
  • Choose a game window: one evening, one dinner, or one full family day.
  • Keep a discard area for failed missions.

Optional Rules

  • No repeating the same mission in one round.
  • Players may trade 1 mission before the game begins.
  • Use bonus points for the funniest successful mission.
  • Play best of 2 rounds if you want a rematch.

1–6

  • Get someone to say “Santa.”
  • Get someone to mention snow.
  • Convince someone to compliment the Christmas tree.
  • Get someone to sing part of a Christmas song.
  • Secretly place a candy cane in someone’s pocket or belongings.
  • Get two people to say “Christmas” within one minute.

7–12

  • Get someone to tell you their favorite Christmas movie.
  • Convince someone to wear a Santa hat.
  • Get someone to mention Rudolph.
  • Make someone laugh while you’re holding an ornament.
  • Get someone to offer you food.
  • Get someone to tell a childhood Christmas story.

13–18

  • Get someone to say “That’s ridiculous.”
  • Convince two people to take a selfie together.
  • Get someone to touch the Christmas tree.
  • Get someone to hum a Christmas song.
  • Get someone to ask what you’re doing.
  • Get someone to mention presents.

19–24

  • Get someone to say the word “cookie.”
  • Convince someone the room is too hot or too cold.
  • Get someone to bring you something red.
  • Get someone to talk about Christmas dinner.
  • Get someone to put on Christmas music.
  • Get someone to take a picture of you.

Christmas Family Feud

30–45 min • Teams of 2–3 or individual • 10 ready-to-play rounds

⌄

How to Play

  1. Read the question aloud.
  2. Teams take turns giving one answer at a time.
  3. If the answer is on the list, reveal its points.
  4. If the answer is not on the list, the other team can steal with one answer.
  5. Highest score at the end wins.

Scoring

5 pts = 1st • 4 pts = 2nd • 3 pts = 3rd • 2 pts = 4th • 1 pt = 5th

Steal Rules

  • If a team gives an answer not on the list, call “X” and let the other team steal.
  • They get one answer only.
  • If it is on the list, they earn the points.
  • If not, move to the next question.

Optional

  • Use a buzzer or festive object, like a jingle bell.
  • Set a 5–10 second answer limit.
  • Play best of 3 or all 10 rounds.

Mystery Stocking

10–20 min • 3–5+ players • Feel it, guess it

⌄

How to Play

  1. Fill a stocking with 6–10 holiday items.
  2. Players take turns reaching inside without looking.
  3. Feel one item and guess what it is by touch alone.
  4. Reveal the item and score 1 point for each correct guess.
  5. Keep going until all items are guessed.
  6. Highest score wins, or just play for laughs.

Ways to Play

Classic
Guess each item one at a time.
Timed Round
10 seconds to guess each item.
Team Play
Teammates may whisper one hint after 5 seconds.
Memory Mode
Feel all items first, then write your guesses.

Setup

  • Choose 6–10 small objects.
  • Use one stocking or gift bag that hides the contents.
  • Keep a score sheet nearby.
  • Optional: use a blindfold for an extra challenge.

Optional Rules

  • No peeking.
  • No shaking the stocking.
  • One guess per item.
  • Hard Mode: use trickier items or give only 10 seconds.
  • Bonus point for a perfect round.

Christmas Password

One word. Big clues. Holiday fun.

⌄

How to Play

  1. Divide into 2 teams or play individually.
  2. One player from the clue-giving team draws a card.
  3. Give one-word clues to help your team guess the word.
  4. You cannot say the word, part of the word, spell it, rhyme it, or use gestures.
  5. Keep going until the team guesses correctly or time runs out.

Rules

  • Use one word at a time.
  • No saying the word or any part of the word.
  • No rhyming.
  • No gestures, acting, or sounds.
  • Pass if needed, optional.
  • Each correct guess = 1 point, or play for time.
  • Set a 60-second timer per card.

Game Variations

  • Timed Round: 1 minute per card.
  • Power Round: no passing.
  • Lightning Round: 30 seconds.
  • Team Rotation: each player gives 2–3 clues.
  • Use more challenging words for older players.
