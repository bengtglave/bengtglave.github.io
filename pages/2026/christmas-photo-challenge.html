12 Photos of Christmas

This year, every household gets a little holiday homework: capture twelve merry moments before the season slips away in a blur of cookies, wrapping paper, and somebody asking where the tape went.

🎅 You better think twice...
Santa isn't only checking his list this year. At the end of the holiday season, he'll be collecting your 12 Photos of Christmas to make sure you actually saved a few of those chaotic, cozy, ridiculous, wonderful memories. No photographic evidence? Very suspicious. 👀

Choose Your Christmas Photo Mission

Pick one of the two themes below. Both lead to twelve photos, but the adventures are a little different. Choose your favorite and start snapping anytime in December.

1

The Classic Christmas Challenge

Specific festive prompts, cozy details, silly faces, and classic holiday snapshots.

Pick this one if you love a good Christmas scavenger hunt. 🎄
2

The Christmas Story Challenge

Broader themes that let you decide what your family's Christmas story looks like this year.

Pick this one if you want more freedom to capture the moment your way. 📸

Santa's Official Fine Print

Choose one theme • Take all 12 photos during December • Phones absolutely count • Perfection is forbidden • Funny outtakes are encouraged • Pets may participate without signing a release • Bring or share your finished collection at the end of the holiday season.
